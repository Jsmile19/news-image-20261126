A 1024 x 1024 news poster for **Thursday, 26 November 2026**, drawn by a Python program from the day's reported news. The lead story is the Israeli strike in Gaza that killed nine people including Hamas's deputy education minister, followed by Russian missiles hitting Kyiv and a grain ship near Odesa, North Korea signalling nuclear expansion, EU leaders bargaining in Brussels over the budget and Ukraine funding, the WHO flagging a new COVID variant, a building collapse in Karachi, and Russia's biggest military budget since the USSR, with smaller news extras in a ticker strip along the bottom. `main.ts` copies the poster to the output directory; `draw_poster.py` redraws it from scratch.
